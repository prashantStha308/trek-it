title Trek-It — Tourist Payment Process

actor Tourist
participant Frontend
participant Payment Service
participant Dodo (Gateway)
actor Guide

Tourist -> Frontend: Click "Pay Now"
Frontend -> Payment Service: POST /payment/init {bookingId, amount}
Payment Service -> Dodo (Gateway): Create payment request
Dodo (Gateway) --> Payment Service: Return checkout_url
Payment Service --> Frontend: Return checkout_url
Frontend --> Tourist: Redirect to Dodo checkout page

Tourist -> Dodo (Gateway): Complete payment
Dodo (Gateway) --> Tourist: Redirect back with payment status

Frontend -> Payment Service: Verify payment status
Payment Service -> Dodo (Gateway): GET /payment/verify
Dodo (Gateway) --> Payment Service: Payment confirmed

Payment Service -> Payment Service: Hold funds in escrow
Payment Service -> Guide: Notify: payment received for booking
Payment Service --> Frontend: Booking confirmed
Frontend --> Tourist: Display invoice
